# Impeccable critique: products page and site design system

Design health score: 28/40.

Overall verdict: The site has a credible premium-lawyer direction. The ocean ink hero, portrait, and restrained dark navigation create trust. The weakest area is that inner pages still use repeated landing-page scaffolding: uppercase eyebrows, large hero blocks, and card matrices. It is polished enough for a first complete version, but not yet distinctive enough to feel like a finished personal brand.

Priority issues:

1. P1: Repeated uppercase eyebrows make the site feel templated. SERVICES, COMPARE, CONTACT, ABOUT, TIMELINE, ARTICLES repeat the same grammar too often. Keep one strong label in the hero, then use quieter section titles or contextual labels.

2. P1: Typography is competent but not yet memorable. The system Chinese sans stack is readable, but the heavy 900 headings across every page flatten the brand voice. A lawyer personal brand can keep system Chinese fonts while using more deliberate weight contrast, smaller inner-page headings, and better line rhythm.

3. P2: Product cards are improved, but still read as four parallel cards rather than a professional service matrix. They need more hierarchy inside each card: service promise first, scenarios second, deliverables third. Current copy is clear but equal-weight.

4. P2: Warm paper background is close to the 2026 AI default. It works because the brief originally asked for beige, but it should be cooler and less creamy, or used only as a content surface under stronger dark brand sections.

5. P2: Inner pages need more differentiated pacing. About, Products, and Articles all begin with similar dark hero blocks and then beige content. The brand would feel more mature if each page had one distinctive structural move.

Strengths:

- The homepage background and portrait composition build trust quickly.
- The service taxonomy is clear and relevant to the target audience.
- Mobile layouts remain readable, with no obvious overflow or collision.

Detector result: Clean. No deterministic Impeccable anti-patterns were found in the scanned Astro pages.

Recommended next pass:

- Reduce repeated section eyebrows.
- Refine typography weight and inner-page scale.
- Make the product/service cards less card-like and more like a premium service matrix.
- Cool the paper tone slightly.
